# 🚀 Week 2: UML Use Case Diagram & Gantt Chart

**📅 Date:** August 21 – August 27, 2026  
**🎯 Core Task:** Analyzed the project requirements, identified user-side use cases, and contributed to the UML Use Case Diagram and Gantt Chart.
---

### 🛠️ Key Contributions & Decisions

- **👤 Actor Identification:** Analyzed the project requirements and identified the **User** as the primary actor interacting with the VR Travel Experience system.

- **🔗 User Use Cases:** Identified the major user-facing functionalities of the system, including:
  - Register / Login
  - Browse Destinations
  - Search Destinations
  - View Destination Details
  - Start VR Tour
  - Add Destination to Favorites
  - View Tour History
  - Exit VR Tour

- **📐 UML Use Case Diagram:** Worked on mapping the identified user requirements to their corresponding use cases and contributed to structuring the UML Use Case Diagram.

- **🔄 Use Case Relationships:** Reviewed how different user actions are connected within the system and helped organize the relationships between the relevant use cases.

- **📊 Gantt Chart Planning:** Contributed to the overall Gantt Chart by identifying and organizing tasks related to **requirements analysis, UML modelling, documentation, and project planning**.

- **👥 Task Distribution:** Helped divide the project activities among the three team members according to their respective areas of work, ensuring that the workload was distributed across VR development, backend/database development, and analysis/planning.

- **📝 Documentation:** Organized the UML and planning information for inclusion in the project's documentation.

---

### 📚 Learning Outcomes

- Learned how to derive actors and use cases from functional requirements.
- Understood how a UML Use Case Diagram represents interactions between users and a system.
- Learned how project activities can be divided into smaller tasks and organized using a Gantt Chart.
- Gained experience in planning and coordinating tasks within a team.

---

### 📎 Deliverables

- UML Use Case Diagram
- Project Gantt Chart

### ✅ Week 2 Outcome

The primary user interactions were identified and modelled in the **UML Use Case Diagram**, while the project's tasks and responsibilities were organized as part of the **Gantt Chart**.
