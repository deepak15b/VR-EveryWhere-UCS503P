# 🚀 Week 2: UML Use Case Diagram & Gantt Chart

**📅 Date:** August 21 – August 27, 2026  
**🎯 Core Task:** Identified VR-specific user interactions and contributed them to the UML Use Case Diagram and Gantt Chart.

---

### 🛠️ Key Contributions & Decisions

- **🥽 VR Requirements:** Analyzed the requirements from Week 1 to identify the interactions specifically related to the VR portion of the system.

- **🗺️ VR Use Cases:** Identified the major VR-related use cases, including:
  - Start VR Tour
  - Explore Destination
  - Navigate VR Environment
  - Interact with Information Hotspots
  - View Destination Information
  - Exit VR Tour

- **🚶 Navigation Planning:** Contributed to defining how users would move through and explore the virtual environment during a VR tour.

- **📍 Information Hotspots:** Identified information hotspots as an important interaction that allows users to access additional information about locations or objects within the virtual destination.

- **📐 UML Use Case Diagram:** Added the VR-specific interactions to the **UML Use Case Diagram** and helped connect them with the primary **User** actor.

- **🔗 Use Case Relationships:** Reviewed how VR actions such as starting a tour, exploring the environment, and interacting with hotspots are connected within the overall user workflow.

- **📊 Gantt Chart:** Contributed VR development tasks to the project Gantt Chart, including **Unity setup, VR environment creation, navigation, interaction design, and hotspot implementation**.

- **🤝 Team Coordination:** Coordinated with the backend and planning work to ensure that the VR features would integrate with the overall system requirements.

---

### 📚 Learning Outcomes

- Learned how to convert VR requirements into UML use cases.
- Understood how users interact with a VR system through different use cases.
- Learned how VR development activities can be divided into smaller tasks for project planning.
- Gained experience in coordinating VR requirements with other system components.

- 📊 Project Gantt Chart
